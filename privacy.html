2. Information we collect

If you contact us through our website or by email, we may collect your name, email address, telephone number, the content of your enquiry and subsequent correspondence. Website hosting providers may also process technical information such as IP addresses and browser-related request logs to provide, secure and maintain the website.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information in the contact form unless it is necessary for your enquiry.

3. Why we use your information

  • To read and respond to enquiries, discuss potential work and take steps you request before entering into a contract. Our lawful basis is generally taking steps at your request before a contract.
  • To manage ongoing business correspondence and maintain appropriate records of communications. Where applicable, we rely on our legitimate interests in administering customer enquiries and business operations.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations, where relevant.
  • To maintain the security and operation of the website, where necessary for our legitimate interests in protecting our website and services.

Our legitimate interests include responding effectively to customers, keeping accurate enquiry records and protecting our systems. We do not use contact form submissions to add you to marketing mailing lists automatically.

4. How information is shared

Our website is hosted by Netlify. Contact form submissions are processed and stored through Netlify Forms so that we can receive and manage enquiries. Relevant information may also be handled by our email provider and other service providers involved in responding to enquiries, subject to appropriate contractual and data protection safeguards.

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information where required by law or when reasonably necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

5. International transfers

Some technology providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where a restricted international transfer takes place, we are responsible for ensuring an appropriate UK data protection transfer mechanism or other lawful safeguard applies. Further details of applicable arrangements can be requested using the contact address above.

6. How long we keep information

For enquiries that do not lead to work or an ongoing customer relationship, we normally retain the enquiry and related correspondence for up to 12 months from the last meaningful contact, after which it is reviewed and deleted unless there is a legitimate reason to retain it longer. Where an enquiry leads to work, records may be kept for a longer period as necessary for contractual, accounting, warranty, dispute-resolution or other legal purposes. We review retained information and delete it when it is no longer needed.

7. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances and the lawful basis for processing, you may have the right to request access to your information, correction of inaccurate information, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability or to object to certain processing. You may also have the right to withdraw consent where consent is the lawful basis, although we do not normally rely on consent just to respond to an enquiry.

To exercise your rights or ask a privacy question, email info@djminstalls.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

8. Cookies and third-party resources

This website does not currently provide advertising or analytics features through its own page code. Hosting infrastructure and third-party resources, including externally supplied fonts, may process technical information when your browser requests them. If additional analytics or non-essential cookies are introduced, we will review the applicable notice and consent requirements.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if our services, suppliers or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
