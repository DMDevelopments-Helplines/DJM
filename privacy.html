PRIVACY INFORMATION

Your enquiry and your data.

When you contact DJM Installs, we collect the information you enter in our contact form, which may include your name, email address, phone number and message. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and to manage related correspondence.

Our website is hosted by Netlify, which processes and stores contact form submissions on our behalf. We retain enquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the enquiry and associated records, subject to legal requirements. We do not sell contact information.

To ask about the information we hold, request a correction or make a privacy request, contact info@djminstalls.co.uk. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Before launch: This is a provisional privacy notice. DJM Installs should confirm the legal entity/controller details, a defined retention schedule and any other providers before publishing its final policy.
